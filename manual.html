1. System Overview & Scope

The Bond & Recovery Institute (BRI) platform provides accredited pre-licensing education, continuing education (CE) compliance tracking, professional gear procurement, and multi-state operational management for surety recovery agents across Missouri, Kansas, Oklahoma, and Arkansas. Powered by pure HTML5, Tailwind CSS, JavaScript, and Supabase, the platform delivers a streamlined, secure digital environment.

2. Student Portal & Navigation

Students authenticate securely into the platform to access their personalized dashboard. Key student features include:

  • Extended Profile Hub: Displays legal names, assigned student IDs (e.g., BRI-10492), agency affiliations, drivers license credentials, and professional background.
  • State CE Compliance Tracker: Real-time tracking of continuing education credit units across MO, KS, OK, and AR jurisdictions.
  • Course Player Launch: Dynamic routing to interactive SCORM training modules (e.g., Ground Zero Foundations, Surety Recovery Operations) via player.html.
  • Verified Certificates: Upon course completion, students can generate official certificates of completion which dynamically pull their legal names and open securely in a new tab (target="_blank") ready for printing or PDF export.

3. E-Commerce Supply Store & Secure Checkout

The equipment catalog (store.html) features authorized badges, tactical vests, statute manuals, searchlights, and handcuffs:

  • Order Cart Badge: Real-time item counter updates as gear or courses are added.
  • Secure Checkout (checkout.html): Calculates totals, simulates secure credit card processing, records completed transactions directly into the Supabase orders table, and auto-confirms course enrollments.

4. Enterprise & Multi-State Integration

The platform links directly with regional enterprise hubs. Most notably, the Live Missouri Enterprise Portal is integrated directly via secure cross-domain routing (https://krystal-bail-bonds.vercel.app/)[cite: 1] for fugitive recovery and agency coordination.

5. Administrative Oversight

Administrators have full visibility into user database records, course catalogs, inventory stock levels, and transaction ledgers managed through Supabase backend security policies (RLS).
